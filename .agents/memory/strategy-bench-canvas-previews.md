---
name: Strategy Bench canvas previews
description: User requirement for the screen size and arrangement of Strategy Bench design concepts on the canvas.
---

Strategy Bench concept frames must use desktop landscape dimensions, not phone portrait dimensions. Arrange the concepts side by side and focus one at a readable scale instead of fitting all three into a zoomed-out overview.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for computer-screen-sized concepts after the stacked portrait previews were too small to inspect.

**How to apply:** When presenting Strategy Bench design concepts, use approximately 1280×800 frames, place them in a horizontal row, and focus a single frame first. Keep the individual Preview controls available for inspecting each option.
