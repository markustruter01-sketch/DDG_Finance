No Fractional Shorts: Alpaca does not allow shorting fractional shares. Update the execution layer to explicitly use math.floor() to round down to whole integers when sizing direct short orders. If the notional value is too low to short 1 whole share, the bot must skip the trade.

Hardcoded Time Exits: In hardcoded_exits.py, enforce a strict 5-trading-day maximum holding period for 3x leveraged inverse ETFs (SPXS, SQQQ, SRTY) to prevent volatility decay drag. 1x inverse ETFs (like SH) do not need this strict time exit.
