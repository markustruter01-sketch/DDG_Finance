The locked split-sample experiment selected MSFT, AAPL, BRK-B, WM, MA, and PG using 2018–2022 opening-gap expectancy. On untouched 2023-present data, positive-surprise PEAD with a 20-session hold was the strongest tested variant and beat both buy-before variants and its matched random control; no live strategy should be inferred from this research result.

**Why:** The validation passed the stated Sharpe, expectancy, random-control, and out-of-sample criteria, but it was an explicitly research-only experiment and is not authorization to build or deploy Bot 2 execution.

**How to apply:** Preserve the mechanical top-six selection and locked parameters when comparing future evidence; do not retune to the observed result or route it into live runners without a separate authorization.
